Behind the craft

A love of wood
and making things.

Alfie's Wood Works is all about the joy of making something by hand. I love the warmth, texture, and individuality of natural wood — and turning it into small things that make lovely gifts.

No two pieces of timber are quite alike. I aim to let that natural character shine through, with care taken over the shape, finish, and details of each creation.
